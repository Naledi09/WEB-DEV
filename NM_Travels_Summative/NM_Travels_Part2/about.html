Our Story

NM Travels was founded in 2024 as a small long-distance transport service, initially using a single 7-seater vehicle between two cities and two airport locations. The business later expanded to two 7-seater vehicles and a 14-seater vehicle to support daily shuttle services, airport transfers and private rides.

The website is designed around a simple principle: passengers should be able to understand where a vehicle is going, when it leaves and what the trip is likely to cost.

Meet our 7-seater and 14-seater vehicles

Mission

To make shared transport dependable, easy to plan around flight times and transparently priced.

Vision

To become the default shuttle option for airport and intercity travel in Limpopo and Gauteng, known for punctuality and reasonable prices.

Who We Serve

Airport travellers, commuters, event planners, corporate clients, hotel guests and private groups.

Service Area

We operate along the N1 between Polokwane and Johannesburg, including Polokwane International Airport and OR Tambo International Airport.
