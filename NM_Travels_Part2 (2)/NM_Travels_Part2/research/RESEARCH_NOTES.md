# NM Travels – Part 1 Research Notes

NM Travels is a fictional small transport business created for an academic website project. The proposed website focuses on airport transfers, intercity shuttle services, weekday commuting and private group transport.

## Target audiences
- Airport travellers wanting a cheaper, predictable alternative to a private cab.
- Weekday commuters using fixed routes.
- Corporate and event clients needing group transport.
- Hotel and convention guests needing scheduled transfers.

## Research themes
The site should prioritise customer convenience, transparent route information, mobile usability and trust. Users should be able to review routes and schedules, submit booking requests, request private charter quotations and see vehicle capacity before travelling.

## Content guidance
The route times, prices, addresses, phone number and email address shown on the website are fictional examples. Before production use, they must be replaced with verified business information.

## Technical planning
HTML and CSS provide structure and presentation. JavaScript is used for the route search demonstration, filtering, navigation behaviour and client-side form validation. A future production version could use a server-side booking system, email/SMS confirmation service and map API.
